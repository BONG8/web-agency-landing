## Design Context

### Users
Potential clients looking to hire a web developer for websites and online services. They are likely reviewing the portfolio to assess technical competence, design sensibility, and professionalism before making a hiring decision.

### Brand Personality
Ambitious, professional, and calm. The voice and tone should reflect a student who is driven yet grounded, offering a reassuring and methodical approach to projects. The interface should evoke a sense of calm confidence.

### Aesthetic Direction
Minimalist, clean, and avant-garde. The design should utilize both light and dark modes with a focus on simplicity, removing unnecessary noise. It should leverage custom typography, unconventional but intuitive navigation, and subtle, interactive micro-animations for storytelling.

### Design Principles
- **Calm Professionalism**: Use generous spacing, refined typography, and smooth transitions to create a relaxed yet highly competent atmosphere.
- **Intentional Minimalism**: Every element must serve a purpose. Remove decorative clutter in favor of impactful, deliberate design choices.
- **Storytelling through Motion**: Use micro-interactions to guide the user naturally through the work without overwhelming them.
- **Avant-Garde Restraint**: Push boundaries with typography and layout, but never at the expense of usability or the "calm" emotional baseline.
